Key takeaways

Editable analyst notes for the deck.

  • Capital is concentrated: the United States and China hold 68 percent of all unicorns, so the least crowded opportunities sit in fast rising markets like India, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.
  • Efficiency beats hype: software and fintech turn one dollar of funding into roughly six dollars of valuation, while capital heavy sectors like auto and transportation lag under three.
  • Mind the 2021 bubble: 48 percent of every unicorn was minted in a single year of cheap capital, so we weight recent, durable, capital efficient signals over raw 2021 valuations.
  • The valuation model works: a tuned gradient boosting model explains 51 percent of held-out valuation variation from funding, sector, geography, and timing, and the 0.57 funding elasticity confirms diminishing returns to capital.
  • Survivorship bias now has a control group: the expanded 75,230-company sample adds current and exited unicorns, soonicorn proxies, and funded non-unicorn controls, plus a four-point valuation panel (2022 to 2026).
